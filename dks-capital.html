DKS Capital investment process

DKS Capital is my personal portfolio, run since July 2020 as a concentrated value strategy. The aim is simple: own a few exceptional businesses, bought at sensible prices, for a long time.

What I look for

SimplicityPredictable businesses inside my circle of competence.
Durable moatCompetitive advantages that resist technological disruption.
Financial strengthFree-cash-flow generative, low leverage, self-financing.
ManagementHonest, capable operators, with a preference for founder-led companies.
PriceA clear margin of safety relative to intrinsic value.

How the portfolio is built

I hold four to six positions and would rather own more of my best ideas than add a twentieth. Each holding has to pass one test: would I be comfortable owning it if the market closed for five to ten years? Volatility is treated as an opportunity to buy businesses I already understand at a discount.

When I sell

Broken thesisThe original reasoning was wrong or no longer holds.
Opportunity costA clearly better business becomes available.
Full valuationThe price reaches or exceeds my estimate of intrinsic value.

Weekly review

Every Monday I run a structured portfolio meeting: year-to-date performance, a review of each holding, upcoming events such as earnings, the market and economic backdrop, and new candidates from my pre-vetted library.
