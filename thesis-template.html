Pre-vetted library: thesis template

Every business enters the library through the same template. The goal is to make research actionable: when a price falls, the decision has already been thought through.

Template

BusinessWhat the company does and how it makes money, in plain language.
MoatThe source of its competitive advantage and why it should last. This is the first filter; no moat, no entry.
ManagementTrack record, incentives, and capital allocation history.
Bull caseWhat has to go right, and what the market is already pricing in (checked with a reverse DCF).
Bear caseThe strongest argument against owning it, written as if by a short seller.
Intrinsic valueA range, with the key assumptions stated.
Buy priceAn explicit price at which the margin of safety is sufficient.
Sizing triggersThe prices or events at which the position starts, grows, or is cut.
Thesis breakersSpecific developments that would prove the thesis wrong.
